CLAUDIA

Owner, Skilled Society & UBL Roofing

Claudia built her name in the trades. As the owner of a roofing company based in New Jersey, she's spent years in an industry that demands precision, accountability, and a relentless work ethic. The same values that make a great contractor make a great brand.


Skilled Society was born from a simple idea: the people who do the real work deserve real gear. Not fast fashion, not logo-heavy hype — something built with the same care that goes into every job site.

The Origin
FROM THE
JOB SITE
TO THE STREETS.

It started on a roof. Running a roofing company in New Jersey means early mornings, hard decisions, and zero room for shortcuts. It also means building something that lasts — and taking pride in the work even when nobody's watching.

That same mindset is what Skilled Society is built on. Every design, every piece, every stitch goes through the same standard: would we put our name on it? If the answer isn't yes, it doesn't ship.

We're not chasing trends. We're not trying to be Supreme or Palace. We're New Jersey. We work for what we have. And now we wear it.

What We Stand For
01
NO SHORTCUTS

Every piece is printed in-house in New Jersey. We don't outsource the craft. We don't cut corners on materials. If it's got our name on it, it earned it.

02
MADE HERE

Jersey isn't just our zip code — it's our identity. Blue-collar, no-nonsense, and real. We didn't move to be cool. We stayed to build something worth wearing.

03
EARNED NOT GIVEN

We make limited drops, not unlimited supply. The people who get it early put in the effort. That's intentional. Scarcity is how we keep it worth something.

04
COMMUNITY FIRST

The society part is real. This is for the people who grind — trades, construction, service workers, small business owners. The people who make places like New Jersey run.
